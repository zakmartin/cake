Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the UK adequacy decision, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Cookies

The Site works without cookies. The only cookies that may be set are those from the Google Ads tag used for advertising measurement. Vercel Web Analytics does not set cookies. MailerLite may set cookies only if you open a hosted form or email link on its domain. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

5. Pinterest

We publish pins about cake pricing and Cake Quote Kit on Pinterest. To do this we use a Pinterest developer application that connects to the Pinterest API on behalf of our own Pinterest business account only.

  • The application publishes and manages our own pins and boards. It does not access, collect or store personal data of other Pinterest users.
  • The application does not request access to any Pinterest user's account other than ours.
  • We do not use the Pinterest tag or Pinterest conversion tracking on the Site.
  • If you interact with our pins on Pinterest (save, comment, follow), that activity is governed by the Pinterest Privacy Policy. Pinterest shows us only aggregated pin statistics.
  • Any Pinterest API data we receive (for example, pin performance statistics) relates to our own content and is used only to improve our pins. We do not sell, transfer, or use it for any other purpose, in accordance with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

6. How long we keep data

  • Newsletter and email result data: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, or after 24 months of inactivity, whichever is sooner.
  • Order and accounting records: as long as required by Czech tax and accounting law (generally 10 years).
  • Support emails: up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Server logs: typically no longer than 30 days.
  • Aggregated analytics: without personal identifiers, kept for statistics.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data erased where there is no legal reason to keep it,
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • receive your data in a portable format,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

To exercise any right, email us at the address in section 1. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz) or with your local supervisory authority.

8. Children

The Site and our Pinterest content are intended for adults running or planning a baking business. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Calculator inputs stay in your browser unless you explicitly request an email result. Email addresses and results are transmitted directly to MailerLite and are not logged or stored on our servers. API credentials for third‑party services are stored as protected environment variables and never in public files.

We may update this policy when the Site or our services change. The current version is always published at cakequotekit.app/privacy.html with the date of the last update at the top.
