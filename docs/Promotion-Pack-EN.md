## Six short videos — screen recordings, no face required

Record vertical 1080×1920, roughly 20–35 seconds. Add readable English captions. Show the calculator at a legible scale rather than shrinking the entire page. The end card points to the link in the profile where the platform supports it; verify account link availability before promising a clickable caption link.

1. **The $65 cake.** Hook: “What does a $65 cake actually pay you?” Show $18 ingredients + $6 overheads + $2.25 example fees. Remaining $38.75 ÷ 3 hours = $12.92/hour. End: “Try your own numbers. Free cake pricing check.” On screen: “Fictional example. Before tax and omitted costs.”
2. **The forgotten three hours.** Hook: “Did you price the ingredients—or the whole cake?” Enter 3 hours at $15 and show $45 labour. Keep other values visible. End: “Your time belongs in the price.”
3. **Margin versus markup.** Hook: “25% margin is not a 25% markup.” Show cost $60, no fees, margin 25%, target $80. End: “Check the calculation before sending the quote.”
4. **One small fee.** Hook: “Have you counted your payment fee?” Show a fictional percentage fee plus fixed fee; explain that the viewer must use their own provider's rates. End on the target price update.
5. **One quote, one shopping list.** Show the actual workbook: ingredient selection, required quantity and shopping list. Caption: “Uses recorded stock. Stock movements entered manually.” End: “Explore the Excel kit.”
6. **Before you hit send.** Show three checklist items: decorating time, packaging, delivery. End: “Open the free checklist. Print it for your next quote.”
